DarkSignal Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

DarkSignal is a news visualization application developed by KE Aps. It displays publicly available news and events on an interactive world map.

1. Information We Collect

DarkSignal does not require user registration, personal profiles, or access to the device's GPS location. The current version does not integrate Firebase Analytics or AdMob advertising.

2. News and Map Services

DarkSignal retrieves publicly available information from news feeds, including RSS sources and GDELT, and displays map content using external map services. Network requests to these providers may expose technical information, such as IP addresses, to the providers under their respective privacy policies.

3. Local Storage

The application may store downloaded news and event information locally on the device to improve loading performance. This information is not associated with a user account.

4. External Links

DarkSignal may open original news articles on third-party websites. These websites operate under their own privacy policies.

5. Advertising and Analytics

The current version does not include advertising or third-party analytics SDKs. If these features are introduced, this policy will be updated before their activation.

6. Children's Privacy

DarkSignal is a general news application and is not specifically directed at children.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the application's features or data practices change. Updates will be published on this page.
